Our Story

Company Background

Synergy Deals Limited was established in 2017 and is duly registered in Kenya under the Companies Act, 2015. In the years since, we have grown from a small local supplier into a trusted partner for corporate, ICT and construction clients across East Africa.

Today, with close to a decade of experience behind us, we supply everything from IT and telecom equipment to office essentials, branding materials, and construction and electrical supplies — all under one reliable roof.
